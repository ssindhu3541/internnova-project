About InternNova

We help students turn learning into real work experience.

Who we are

InternNova is a student-first internship platform. We combine guided internships, practical courses, live workshops and mentor reviews so students can build real projects before they graduate.

Our mission

To make practical work experience available to every student, regardless of college, city or background.

What we offer

  • Eight internship tracks: AI & ML, Web Development, Data Analytics, Cloud & DevOps, UI/UX Design, Cyber Security and App Development.
  • Video courses and live workshops with working engineers.
  • A weekly task schedule with a progress percentage.
  • Verified certificates for completed internships.

Our values

  • Learn by doing. Every skill is practised in a real project.
  • Honest feedback. Mentors tell you what to improve.
  • Open to everyone. Beginners are welcome.
