Privacy policy

How InternNova collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. What we collect

When you register we ask for your name, email address, college and interested domain. We also record your activity inside the platform, such as tasks completed, applications and course progress.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and show your dashboard.
  • To match you with internships, courses and workshops.
  • To issue your certificate.
  • To send updates you have asked for.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with mentors and partner organisations involved in your internship, or when required by law.

4. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account data and unsubscribe from emails at any time.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Please keep your password private.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, write to privacy@internnova.example (replace with your real address).
